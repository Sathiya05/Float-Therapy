Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know before your float therapy session.

What is float therapy? +

Float therapy is a relaxation technique where you float in warm saltwater to eliminate external stimulation and achieve deep mental and physical relaxation.

Is float therapy safe? +

Yes, it is completely safe. The water contains Epsom salt and is hygienically filtered after every session.

How long is a float session? +

Sessions typically last between 60 to 90 minutes depending on your selected package.

Do I need experience to try float therapy? +

No experience is required. First-time users are guided before the session for comfort and ease.

What should I bring? +

Nothing is required. Towels, earplugs, shampoo, and essentials are provided at the center.

Can I float if I have injuries or pain? +

Yes, float therapy helps reduce muscle tension and joint pain, but consult your doctor for serious conditions.

Will I feel claustrophobic inside the tank? +

No. You control light and sound. Many users feel calm and relaxed within minutes.

How often should I do float therapy? +

Once a week or twice a month is ideal for stress relief and mental wellness.

Can I fall asleep inside the tank? +

Yes, many people fall into deep relaxation or light sleep safely during the session.

What are the main benefits of float therapy? +

It reduces stress, improves sleep, enhances creativity, relieves pain, and promotes deep mental clarity.
