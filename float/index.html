What is Float Therapy?

Float therapy is a deeply relaxing experience where you float effortlessly in warm water filled with Epsom salt. This creates a zero-gravity environment that helps your body and mind completely unwind.

With minimal sensory input, your brain enters a meditative state, reducing stress, improving focus, and promoting overall wellness.

1 hour of floating = 4–6 hours of deep rest
